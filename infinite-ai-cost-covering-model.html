Infinite AI — cost-covering stake

Sizing the stake so its staking yield actually pays the real GPU cost of serving the tier — instead of the original 6%-of-nominal-credit basis, which understated the requirement by ~60–155×.

01 · The three stacked gaps

Where the original model goes wrong

The original deck's arithmetic is internally correct, but it only discloses one of three gaps between the stake's yield and the true cost of service. They multiply.

GapWhat it isFactor (Pro)
A — multiplierDuration/NFT multiplier reduces the deposit, so yield funds only 1/M of nominal credit3.5×
B — cost basisReal GPU cost ($44.44/M tok) vs nominal credit price, at 50% util44.4×
C — utilizationFull allowance use vs the 50% assumptionup to 2×
CombinedYield vs real cost at headline settings (1,843 0G)~155×
Headline reality The deck's "1,843 0G = Infinite AI" stakes $571 of 0G. At 6% that yields $2.86/month — against a $444/month real cost to serve a Pro user. The stake's yield covers 0.64% of cost.

02 · The corrected formula

required_stake_0G =
   tier_tokens/mo × utilization × $44.44/M × 12   (annual real GPU cost)
   ÷ yield_rate                                    (yield you commit to funding)
   ÷ $0.31                                         (0G price)

Rule: multipliers reward points only — they must NOT reduce staked principal.
